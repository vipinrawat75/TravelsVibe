Chakrata Kanatal Tehri Tour Package

8N / 9D

Discover the breathtaking beauty of Tehri Lake with our specially designed Tehri Lake Tour Package in Uttarakhand. Nestled amidst the majestic Himalayan mountains, Tehri Lake is one of the most scenic tourist destinations in Uttarakhand, famous for its crystal-clear waters, panoramic views and the iconic Tehri Dam. Experience the perfect blend of adventure and relaxation as you enjoy thrilling water sports like jet skiing, boating, kayaking and banana rides, along with peaceful moments surrounded by nature’s serenity. Whether you are planning a family vacation, honeymoon trip, weekend getaway, or an adventure tour in Uttarakhand, Tehri Lake offers an unforgettable experience for every traveler. Our Tehri Lake holiday package lets you explore the natural charm, stunning landscapes and exciting activities that make this destination a must-visit in Uttarakhand tourism. Escape the busy city life and immerse yourself in the tranquil beauty of Tehri Lake for a memorable Himalayan retreat.

Top Attractions

✅ Tehri Dam: One of the tallest dams in India, offering breathtaking lake views and exciting water sports activities.

✅ New Tehri: A beautiful hill town known for panoramic Himalayan views, peaceful surroundings and Tehri Lake tourism.

✅ Dhanaulti: A serene hill station near Mussoorie famous for eco parks, dense forests and pleasant weather throughout the year.

✅ Tiger Falls: A stunning natural waterfall surrounded by lush greenery, perfect for trekking, sightseeing and photography.

✅ Deoban: A hidden paradise for nature lovers, offering dense deodar forests, mountain views, bird watching and peaceful trekking trails.

✅ Budher Caves: Ancient limestone caves popular for adventure trekking and exploring mysterious cave formations in Uttarakhand.

✅ Mindrolling Monastery: One of the largest Buddhist monasteries in India, admired for its magnificent architecture, peaceful ambiance and spiritual significance.

✅ Tapkeshwar Temple: A famous cave temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, known for its natural water droplets falling on the Shivling.

✅ Forest Research Institute (FRI): An iconic colonial-era institute in Dehradun, famous for its grand architecture, museums and lush green campus.

Tour Overview

Day 1

Dehradun Sightseeing

Upon arrival in Dehradun, begin your sightseeing tour by visiting some of the city’s most popular attractions, including the scenic Robber’s Cave, the famous Tapkeshwar Temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, and the peaceful Mindrolling Monastery, known for its beautiful architecture and spiritual ambiance. Enjoy the pleasant atmosphere of Dehradun while exploring its natural beauty and cultural landmarks.

Day 2

Dehradun to Rishikesh (45 Kms / 1.5 Hrs)

After breakfast, drive towards Rishikesh, the Yoga Capital of India, known for its spiritual charm and scenic beauty. Explore famous attractions such as the iconic Laxman Jhula, Ram Jhula and the sacred Triveni Ghat. In the evening, witness the mesmerizing Ganga Aarti on the banks of the holy River Ganga, a truly peaceful and spiritual experience.

Day 3

Rishikesh - Tehri (85 Kms – 3 Hrs)

After breakfast, proceed towards the stunning Tehri Lake, surrounded by breathtaking Himalayan views and peaceful landscapes. Upon arrival, enjoy exciting boating and thrilling water sports activities at one of Uttarakhand’s most popular adventure destinations. Spend the day relaxing amidst the scenic beauty and serene atmosphere of Tehri Lake.

Day 4

Tehri to Kanatal (30 Kms / 1 Hr)

After breakfast, drive towards Kanatal, a peaceful hill station surrounded by dense forests and stunning Himalayan views. Upon arrival, check in to your camp or hotel and relax amidst the serene natural beauty. Later, enjoy thrilling adventure activities at the Kanatal Adventure Camp, including rock climbing, rappelling, valley crossing, and trekking. Spend the evening enjoying the cool mountain weather and peaceful surroundings.

Day 5

Kanatal Local Sightseeing

After breakfast, explore the scenic attractions of Kanatal, known for its lush greenery and panoramic mountain landscapes. Visit nearby spots such as Surkanda Devi Temple and Kaudia Forest for nature walks, photography and sightseeing. Spend the rest of the day at leisure enjoying the calm atmosphere and beautiful Himalayan views before returning to your hotel for an overnight stay.

Day 6

Kanatal to Mussoorie (46 Kms / 2 Hrs)

Drive to Dhanaulti and explore its peaceful atmosphere with visits to Eco Park and Surkanda Devi Temple. Later, proceed to Mussoorie, the Queen of Hills and visit popular attractions like Kempty Falls, Gun Hill, Camel’s Back Road and Mall Road. Stay Overnight stay in Mussoorie.

Day 7

Mussoorie to Chakrata (85 Kms / 3 Hrs)

After breakfast, drive towards Chakrata, a peaceful hill station surrounded by dense forests and scenic Himalayan views. Upon arrival, explore popular attractions like Tiger Falls and Chilmiri Neck, known for their natural beauty and breathtaking landscapes.

Day 8

Chakrata Local Sightseeing

After breakfast, visit the beautiful Mundali Meadows, famous for lush greenery, panoramic mountain views and peaceful surroundings. Spend the day enjoying nature, photography and relaxing in the serene atmosphere of Chakrata.

Day 9

Return to Dehradun (123 Kms / 4 Hrs)

Drive back to Dehradun Your memorable journey ends here. Depart from Dehradun for your onward journey.
